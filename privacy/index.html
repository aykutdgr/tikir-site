Tikir — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 · Developer: Aykut Doğru · Contact: info@ad-apps.com

Summary

Tikir is a gear puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics, and we do not run servers that receive your data. The only third party that may collect data in the app is Google AdMob, which shows ads.

Data stored on your device

  • Your progress (current level, solved levels), settings (sound, vibration) and coin wallet are stored only on your device.
  • This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Coin packs, Ad-Free and the Tikir Club subscription are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only receives Apple's confirmation of what was bought, to give you the content. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The app shows ads with the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions and diagnostic information, to show, measure and improve ads.
  • Before ads start, the app asks for consent where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK) using Google's consent form, and asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. If you do not allow tracking, ads are not personalised with the advertising identifier.
  • You can change your ad consent at any time from the app's Settings (“Ad privacy options”, shown where applicable) and change tracking permission in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Buying Ad-Free or joining Tikir Club removes ads between levels. Optional reward videos are only shown when you choose to watch one.
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

Tikir is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: info@ad-apps.com.
