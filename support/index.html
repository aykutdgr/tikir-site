Tikir — Support

Questions, problems or feedback: info@ad-apps.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Restore purchases

Ad-Free and Tikir Club are tied to your Apple ID. On a new device or after reinstalling, open the map (Workshops) › Settings (gear button) › Restore purchases. Coins bought with money are stored on your device and are not restored after deleting the app.

Manage or cancel Tikir Club

In the app: Settings › Manage subscription. Or on your device: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Tikir. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Report an inappropriate ad

Settings › Report an inappropriate ad, or write to us with a short description and the time you saw it.
